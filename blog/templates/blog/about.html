About Amani

Empowering Communities Through Environmental Awareness

The Problem We're Solving

Environmental crises like floods, water contamination, wildfires, and ecosystem decline are affecting communities worldwide, yet individual voices often go unheard. When disasters strike or environmental issues emerge, people lack a centralized platform to share their experiences, track ongoing incidents, and connect with others facing similar challenges.

Silenced Voices

Communities affected by environmental issues struggle to make their stories heard beyond local boundaries.

Disconnected Communities

People facing similar environmental challenges remain isolated, unable to share solutions or support each other.

Delayed Response

Critical environmental data and real-time updates are scattered across multiple sources, delaying community response.

Our Solution

Amani (meaning "peace" in Swahili) is a community-powered platform that bridges the gap between environmental data and human experience. We provide a space where individuals can share real stories about floods, water contamination, invasive species, and declining ecosystems while accessing comprehensive dashboards tracking environmental incidents across Manitoba.

Community Stories

Share and read firsthand accounts of environmental challenges affecting real people in real communities.

Live Dashboard

Access real-time data on wildfires, floods, and environmental incidents with interactive maps and analytics.

Incident Linking

Connect your posts directly to tracked incidents, creating a comprehensive narrative of environmental events.

Global Network

Join a worldwide community of environmental advocates working together to amplify voices and demand change.

Our Mission

To empower communities worldwide by providing a platform that combines data-driven environmental monitoring with authentic human stories, fostering awareness, connection, and action in the face of environmental challenges.

We believe that every voice matters. By connecting individual experiences with comprehensive environmental data, we create a powerful narrative that can drive policy change, community action, and environmental justice.

Our Vision

A world where environmental challenges are met with immediate community awareness, collaborative problem-solving, and swift action. Where no community faces environmental crisis alone, and every story contributes to a larger movement for planetary health.

Through Amani, we're building bridges between data and humanity, between isolation and community, between silence and action.

What We Track

Amani focuses on critical environmental issues affecting communities across Manitoba and beyond

Wildfires

Monitor active fires, air quality impacts, and community evacuation updates.

Water Quality

Track contamination incidents, boil water advisories, and water safety concerns.

Flooding

Real-time flood monitoring, water levels, and community impact reports.

Invasive Species

Document and track the spread of invasive species affecting local ecosystems.

Marine Life

Report on declining fish populations and aquatic ecosystem health.

Ecosystem Decline

Share observations of habitat loss, biodiversity reduction, and environmental degradation.
